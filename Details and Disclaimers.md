# Enterprise-Network-Design-Project-Campus-Area-Network
===============================================================================================

**DETAILS OF THE PROJECT**

This project is a campus area network design project. It consists of **Technical Goals**, **Business Goals**, **Logical Design** and **Physical Design**, which represents a network design of a university campus area network inspired by the University of New Haven. 

These above-mentioned objectives are achieved while covering the almost identical detailed network layout of the University of New Haven. This was achieved by thorough research on the physical layout of the University of New Haven. Security features are less identical as compared to the original network architecture for security reasons. 
Attention to detail was given to buildings on different campuses and the rooms inside them.
Based on the business plan and technical goals, the network is capable of providing service to about 2000-2500 users at a time.
To add to the safety, this design implements firewalls, IDS, IPS and DMZs for the security of the network.
Additionally, to ensure smooth working even in case a critical network component fails, there are backups available for all the network critical systems.
Also, to protect the system in case of a natural disaster, there is a Disaster Recovery Plan to ensure that the data can be recovered. The DRP is located at a different location that is far away from the location of the campus.
During the development of physical design, the devices required for the plan were chosen based on the probable number of users a particular classroom or office would have. For seamless streaming, Fiber optic cables are used for the communication between the buildings on the main campus and the Orange campus. For communication inside the building, Ethernet Cat6 cables were used and for DRP Ethernet CAT5 cables were used.
